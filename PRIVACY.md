**最后更新日期 / Last Updated**: 2026年3月

## 中文版

### 概述

Markdown Reader（以下简称"本应用"）尊重并保护用户隐私。本隐私政策说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

### 信息收集

本应用未集成账户、使用分析或广告服务，也不会将书架或文档主动上传到应用服务器。具体而言：

- 不收集姓名、邮箱等个人信息
- 不收集设备标识符
- 不收集使用统计数据
- 不为应用使用分析设置 Cookie 或追踪标识
- 不需要用户注册或登录

### 本地存储

本应用仅在您的设备本地存储以下数据：

- 阅读进度
- 应用设置（主题、字体大小等）
- 书架信息（文件标题、路径、最后阅读时间等元数据）
- 无法取得原始文件路径时导入的本地缓存副本

这些数据完全存储在您的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。

### 文件访问

本应用需要访问您选择打开的 Markdown 文件（.md、.markdown、.txt），仅用于阅读和编辑。我们不会上传或分享这些文件。

### 第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方分析、广告或社交服务。文档可能包含远程图片等网络资源；显示这些资源时，会直接连接相应服务，服务提供方可能收到 IP 地址、请求地址等常规网络信息。点击网页链接时，应用会在系统浏览器中打开该地址，后续数据处理适用目标网站的政策。

### 儿童隐私

本应用适合所有年龄段用户使用，不收集任何年龄段的个人信息。

### 政策变更

如本政策有变更，我们将在此页面更新。

### 联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：

**邮箱**：support@markdown-reader.app

## English Version

### Overview

Markdown Reader (hereinafter "the App") respects and protects user privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

### Information Collection

The App has no account, analytics or advertising integration and does not upload your library or documents to an application server. Specifically:

- No personal information such as name or email
- No device identifiers
- No usage statistics
- No cookies or tracking identifiers for application analytics
- No user registration or login required

### Local Storage

The App only stores the following data locally on your device:

- Reading progress
- App settings (theme, font size, etc.)
- Bookshelf metadata, including file titles, paths and last-read timestamps
- Local cached copies of imported files when their original paths are unavailable

This data is stored entirely on your device and is not uploaded to any server.

### File Access

The App needs access to Markdown files (.md, .markdown, .txt) that you choose to open, solely for reading and editing purposes. We do not upload or share these files.

### Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising or social services. Documents may reference remote images and other network resources. Displaying these resources contacts their providers, which may receive normal request information such as your IP address and the requested URL. Web links open in your system browser; the destination site handles subsequent data under its own policy.

### Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages and does not collect personal information from any age group.

### Policy Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page.
